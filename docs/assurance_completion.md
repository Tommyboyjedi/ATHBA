The policy distinguishes acceptance of demonstrated behavior from confidence in non-behavioral assurance. PROVEN remains mechanical PASS; VIOLATED remains blocking FAIL; UNPROVEN never becomes YES. A source-grounded atomic assurance with a known policy but unsupported proof capability may permit qualified completion. Compound/invalid-provenance facts, failed behavioral reconciliation, exhausted corrections, provider/protocol failures, and explicit violations remain blocking. Positive persistence is observable behavior and cannot use this exception.

Qualified completion continues through Naming/Refactor with immutable accepted tests, revision/path controls and independent candidate Gatekeeper unchanged. It is not full specification assurance and must never be reported as unqualified success.
